# Starfall Vengeance — Production Rebuild

## Exactly three modes

1. **Campaign** — 30 waves, boss every 5, full completion goal.
2. **Endless** — adaptive pressure scaled against survival time (capped so
   late runs stay playable).
3. **Gauntlet** — 8 timed trials with rotating combat mutators and a final
   boss trial.
